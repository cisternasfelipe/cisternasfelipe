### What I'm building

**NFC cards with digital profiles** — `laicu.cl`
Physical NFC cards that resolve to a digital profile the owner edits themselves.

The interesting constraint: cards are manufactured with the URL burned into the NFC chip and printed as a QR **before anyone knows who will buy them**. That URL can never change. So cards ship generic and are claimed later with a single-use key issued when payment clears. Card identity and profile identity are separate entities, which means one owner can point several cards at different profiles, and editing a profile never breaks a card already in someone's wallet.

**Barbershop management system** — `barber.laicu.cl` *(in progress)*
Bookings and admin panel for barbershops.

**Organizate** — goal tracking and study-hour logging. TypeScript.
